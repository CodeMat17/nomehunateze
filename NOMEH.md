# Nomeh Unateze Community Website — Feature & Content Structure

## Project Vision

The website should serve as a **digital heritage platform, community information hub, cultural archive, and living history of Nomeh Unateze, Nkanu East LGA, Enugu State**.

It should preserve the community's history, culture, people, geography, traditions, leadership, development activities, media, and stories for present and future generations.

---

# Content Authenticity & Preservation Principles

Because much of the website will rely on oral history and community memory, the platform should clearly distinguish between:

- Verified historical records
- Oral traditions
- Personal recollections
- Community submissions
- Editorially verified information

Historical and cultural content should be reviewed by designated community custodians before being presented as established fact.

The long-term objective is to make the website the **digital memory, cultural archive, and living story of Nomeh Unateze**.
